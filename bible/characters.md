# Characters

## Zayn — camel, Arabic-lead
- **Personality:** calm, patient, steady — never rushes, keeps trying. Teaches patience/persistence.
- **World:** desert/outdoor.
- **Color:** warm sandy/tan base + turquoise accent (woven blanket/scarf, Bedouin/Gulf textile reference). Turquoise = dominant recognition hue.
- **Shape:** big round head, big low-set eyes, small chin, round body, no sharp edges (baby-schema + easy Rigify topology).

## Milo — budgie/parakeet, English-lead
- **Personality:** bubbly, chatty, curious, tries everything right away but gets overwhelmed by big/new things. Teaches excitement-for-new-things + naming big feelings. Chattiness drives the bilingual word-swapping play.
- **World:** domestic/indoor.
- **Color:** bright yellow dominant (real budgie color) + blue/green wing accents.
- **Shape:** same baby-schema formula as Zayn — big round head, big low eyes, small body.

## Relationship
Best friends, live next door (not siblings — preserves the desert/indoor world split, mirrors real preschooler peer friendships). "Two friends from two worlds/languages" is the show's core lesson, not just flavor.

## Engine
Big/calm/outdoor (Zayn) vs. small/quick/indoor (Milo) contrast drives simple one-lesson-per-episode plots, Daniel Tiger-style (one skill per episode, stated simply, repeated/sung).
