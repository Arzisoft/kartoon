# World

Two neighboring homes, one street. Simple, safe, predictable — no scary/surprising elements (toddler 2-5 rule).

## Zayn's side — desert/outdoor
Warm sand tones, open space, simple shapes (dunes, a palm, a woven rug/tent). Bedouin/Gulf textile accents (turquoise) for color pop.

## Milo's side — domestic/indoor
A cozy home interior, bright primary-color furniture/toys, birdcage-as-home-base visual anchor.

## The street between them
Where episodes happen — the shared space both characters cross into. Visually splits the frame: sand tones left, bright indoor tones right, meeting in the middle.

## Language rule
Zayn's world/actions narrated in Arabic, Milo's in English; shared moments (playing together, resolving the episode's problem) said in both, back-to-back — repetition is the teaching mechanism.
